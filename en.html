Privacy Policy – Needlepath

Last updated: 16 August 2026

This privacy policy explains which data is processed, and for what purpose, when you use the mobile application "Needlepath" (the "app") and when you visit this information page. This is a translation of the German privacy policy provided for convenience; in case of discrepancies, the German version prevails.

The short version first: Needlepath is an offline puzzle game. The app requires no account, shows no ads, contains no analytics or tracking services and operates no server infrastructure of its own. Personal data is processed in exactly one, optional case: cloud save via Google Play Games (section 4).

2. Provision of this page (hosting)

This privacy policy is served as a static web page via the GitHub Pages service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the page, GitHub processes technically required connection data – in particular your device's IP address – in server logs in order to deliver the page and to ensure the security and stability of the service. The page itself sets no cookies and embeds no analytics services.

The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing this information reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Processing may take place in the USA; GitHub, Inc. is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the EU Commission under Art. 45 GDPR is in place. For more information, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

3. Data stored locally

Your game progress – the highest unlocked level, your score, and per level the best result, stars, gems and the number of tries and restarts – is stored locally on your device only. It leaves the device only through the channels described in sections 4 and 5.

You can delete the local game progress at any time: in the Android settings under Apps → Needlepath → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Optional cloud save via Google Play Games

If a Google Play Games profile is set up on your device, the app signs in to Google Play Games Services automatically at startup, without a dialog of its own (provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland, or Google LLC). In that case:

  • your game progress (the data from section 3) is stored as a "Saved Game" on Google's servers, linked to your Google Play Games profile, so it survives a new or second device;
  • the app reads the display name of your Play Games profile. It is held in memory only and is neither stored permanently nor shared with anyone.

This cloud save is optional: without a Play Games profile the app runs fully offline and transmits no data at all. Needlepath has no leaderboards, achievements or any other data visible to third parties; the saved game is accessible to you alone.

You can delete saved games by removing the Play Games data or your Play Games profile in your Google account's data management (see Google's guide at support.google.com/googleplay/answer/6260245). Alternatively, a message to support@web-ascend.com is sufficient; we will then assist you with the deletion.

5. Android device backup

The app permits Android device backups ("Auto Backup"). If you have enabled device backup to a Google account in your Android settings, Android may include the local game progress in that backup. This is controlled entirely by your device's backup settings.

6. Permissions

The app requests a single Android permission: Internet. It is used exclusively for the cloud save described in section 4. Without a signed-in Play Games profile the app makes no network connections.

7. No advertising, no analytics

The app contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics or tracking services and no third-party crash reporting services. No advertising ID is processed, and no usage profiles are created.

8. Legal bases (EU/EEA)

For users in the European Economic Area:

  • Cloud save via Google Play Games: processing is carried out to perform the user agreement or at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) – it only takes place if a Play Games profile is set up on your device.
  • Provision of this website: processing is based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), see section 2.

9. Recipients and third parties

The only recipient of the data described in section 4 is Google (Google Play Games Services). Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. Transfers to third countries outside the EEA – in particular the USA – are possible. Google LLC is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for which an adequacy decision of the EU Commission under Art. 45 GDPR is in place.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Beyond that, no data is transmitted to, sold to or shared with third parties; the controller itself receives no personal data from the app.

10. Children

The app is aimed at a general audience and not specifically at children under 13. No personal data of children is knowingly collected; beyond the cloud save described above, no data processing takes place at all, and no advertising is served.

Parents and guardians who have reason to believe that their child has provided personal data can contact support@web-ascend.com at any time. The data concerned will then be deleted.

11. Your rights

Within the scope of the law, you have the right to:

  • access to the data processed about you
  • rectification of inaccurate data
  • erasure of your data
  • restriction of processing
  • objection to processing
  • data portability

To exercise these rights, a message to support@web-ascend.com is sufficient. Independently of this, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

Please note that data processed by Google as its own controller must be addressed directly to Google – most easily via your Google account's data management (see section 4).

12. Storage periods

Data stored locally on the device remains there until you uninstall the app or clear the app data. Saved games backed up via Google Play Games remain until you delete them (see section 4). The hosting server logs are subject to GitHub's retention periods.

13. Data security

The app's communication with Google Play Games uses transport-encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). No server infrastructure of our own stores any users' personal data; this information page is served via GitHub Pages (see section 2).

14. Changes to this privacy policy

This privacy policy will be updated if the app or the underlying legal situation changes. The version published here applies. The current date is stated at the top of this page (16 August 2026).
